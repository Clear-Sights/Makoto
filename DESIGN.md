## Definitions (our construction from the frame)

D1. Step: a Write, Edit, MultiEdit, NotebookEdit, Bash git commit/push, or final answer at Stop (including SubagentStop and PreDelivery); its authored text is content/new_string/new_source, each edits[].new_string, commit -m/--message arguments, or last_assistant_message, respectively. Writer destinations, old_string, command syntax, and protocol keys are not authored assertions. Owner: claims.authored_text. [F3, F8]
D2. Record: the ordered earlier hook events for one session, with admission decisions, paired tool identities and returned bytes; the proposed step is outside its earlier record. Owner: provenance.Ledger. [F3, D1]
D3. Own answer: assistant prose, worker replies, writer acknowledgments, and bytes read from files the session itself wrote; a program's response is distinct from its written program. Owner: Ledger.ingest source classification. [F8, D2]
D4. Reading: a user-provided source statement, or the returned source bytes of a successful matched admitted tool result, excluding an own answer. A result is unsuccessful when its event is PostToolUseFailure, its exit_code/exitCode/exit is nonzero, or isError/is_error is true; an unsuccessful result returns no source bytes. A worker reply (Task or subagent result) is an own answer [D3] even when it quotes a source: "answered from an answer, not the source" [F8]. Any tool may read: Read, Grep, Glob, Bash, WebFetch, WebSearch and MCP tools alike. A direct readback can establish the state of its own file, but cannot establish independent origin for literals copied out of that file. A typed command argument is not a reading. Owner: Ledger.ingest. [F8, F10, D2, D3]
D5. Subject: the existing thing named by a prior record or located outside a step by a path, URL, host, branch, package, service or identifier. Paths use normalized record identity; different paths identify the same subject only through a prior host-recorded subject/alias relation. Equal basenames alone are not identity. Literal spellings retain their bytes. Owner: observed.identity and points.located. [F5, D1, D2]
D6. Point: the subject's path plus explicitly recorded host, branch, environment, copy or target coordinates, and its position in the record; different paths or coordinates are different points. Owner: points.located. [F6, D2, D5]
D7. Claim: a span asserting a subject's value, behavior, place, time or definition. Write/Edit content stating a fact about an existing thing is a claim exactly as final prose is; a span that also creates something remains a claim for its existing reference. Owner: claims.claims. [F3, F8, D1, D5]
D8. Creation: a span introducing a new thing while asserting nothing about a subject; a new program's locally introduced names and values are creations, while its references to prior things or external locations remain claims. No check holds a creation or an assertion-free step. Owner: claims.claims, using syntactic program forms and earlier record membership. [F4, D5, D7]
D9. Literal: the exact token in a claim: number and unit, version, path, URL, identifier, hash, quoted string, or branch, host, environment, copy or target name. A name written after a coordinate kind from D6 is that coordinate’s literal. Neighboring prose never joins the token: "returns v4.3" contains v4.3, not the preceding verb. Owner: claims.literals, reusing precision.names. [D6, D7]
D10. Last change: the greatest recorded position of a subject mutation; a pending mutation reserves that subject until completion or explicit no_effect. An unchanged subject has position zero. Owner: Ledger.mutations and reservations. [F3, D2, D5]
D11. Run: an admitted input selecting an executable subject, paired with its returned response after that subject's last change. Failure output or a completed silent exit is a response, except exit status 126 or 127, where the subject was never executed or found; an unresolved background launch, typed invocation, different subject, or readback is not. Owner: Ledger.executions and run_witnesses. [F7, D2, D5, D10]

## Purpose and postulates

D12. Makoto's purpose is to hold a dependent claim missing its necessary recorded evidence before delivery. Its subordinate purposes are lineage (source origin), spec (reading the thing and its recorded definition), other point (reading where and when used), switch (selected execution and response), ledger (retain ordered evidence), and hook (enforce the resulting hold). [F1, F3, F5, F6, F7, F8, D1–D11]
D13. Postulate: decisions use form and record presence only, without a model. Native syntax and host-owned observations establish the evidence available to this checker; arbitrary prose semantics cannot be proved by the record. The target remains the stated bar, not a claim of universal achievement. [F2, F9, D2, D7]
D14. Postulate: every claim is offered independently to all four checks; each check owns one evidence question, never another check's verdict. Missing evidence can produce more than one independent finding. Creations require no receipt. [F4, F12, D7, D8, D12, D13]

## The four checks: trigger, decision, end

D15. **a, lineage** — "answered from an answer, not the source; needs: the readings themselves: what was read before the write". Trigger: a D1 PreToolUse or final event. Project D7 and D9 once; compare each literal with prior original returned bytes or the identity of the directly read subject. Fire exactly when that literal has no such origin; a readback establishes only its own file identity. Do not fire for a creation, no literals, or a literal with an origin. Historical origin is not a freshness decision. End: rule a finding or empty findings. Implementation: plugin/makoto2/evaluate.py:27, lineage. [F8, F10, D4, D8, D9, D14]
D16. **b, spec**: "the thing isn't what its definition says; needs: one reading, and a definition the checker already holds". Trigger: the same D1 events. Select each subject a claim names [D5]: a path, a URL or an identifier. Fire exactly when that subject has no reading [D4]: a path is read by a reading of that path; a URL by a fetch of it or a search result that returns it; an identifier by a reading whose returned bytes contain it. A definition the checker already holds is a host makoto.definitions entry; such a subject also needs its definition's source read. Without a held definition, a read definition file is an ordinary reading. Do not fire when each selected subject has a reading, or when there is no selected claim. End: rule b finding or empty findings. Implementation: plugin/makoto2/evaluate.py, spec. [F5, D4, D5, D7, D14, D15]
D17. **c, other point**: "true where made, not where it landed; true then, not now; true here, not there; needs: a second reading of the same thing; no definition held". Trigger: the same D1 events. Compare a claimed point [D6] with prior readings [D4] and runs [D11] of that subject. Fire exactly when readings or runs of the subject exist but none is at the claimed point after its last change [D10]. A reading or run by any tool at that point after the last change pays. Related readings are selected only by subject identity or a host alias relation. With no prior reading, the absence belongs to a or b, not c. End: rule c finding or empty findings. Implementation: plugin/makoto2/evaluate.py, other; plugin/makoto2/points.py, other_point. [F6, D4, D6, D9, D10, D11, D14, D15, D16]
D18. **d, switch**: "a branch nothing ever selected; needs: an act first: feed an input, then read the response". Trigger: the same D1 events. Select (i) a claim stating what a subject does when run or fed (its output, response, return or behavior), and (ii) a session change to code or configuration that this step ships (commit, push or final answer). A value claim ("accepts X", "is recorded as X") is not selected; it belongs to a and b. Fire exactly when a selected subject has no successful run [D11] after its last change. A run by any tool that executes the subject and returns its response pays; a worker's report of a run is an answer [D4] and does not. A readback never substitutes for a run. End: rule d finding or empty findings. Implementation: plugin/makoto2/evaluate.py, switch; plugin/makoto2/provenance.py, run_witnesses. [F7, D1, D4, D5, D8, D10, D11, D14]
D19. The evaluator concatenates a, b, c, d findings in that order and deduplicates identical (rule, subject) keys. No decision function mutates evidence or gates another. Disabling one function must leave other findings byte-identical on every built pair. End: findings plus a provenance snapshot where readings exist. [F12, D14, D15, D16, D17, D18]

## Ledger and hook: stages and skin

D20. Ledger trigger: every replayed/admitted event. Stages: assign order and turn; reserve admitted pre-call effects; pair a post-call with its exact tool_use_id/tool_name/tool_input; apply mutations or no_effect; classify original/own response; record locations and executions. Unpaired/replayed/mismatched results become unknown records, not evidence. No stage decides a family hold. End: updated ledger. [D2, D3, D4, D6, D10, D11, D19]
D21. Ledger input skin: hook_event_name, session_id, cwd, tool_name, tool_use_id, tool_input, tool_response, prompt, content, last_assistant_message; host makoto.turn_id, effects[].subject/removed, no_effect, reads[].subject/complete/producer/role, place, definitions[].subject/definition, aliases[].subject/alias, invocation.subject/subjects, external_subjects, network_subjects, detio_store; DETIO_STORE_DIR is the fallback store. Tool-input subfields are file_path, notebook_path, path, content, new_string, old_string, new_source, edits, command, url, query, script, offset, limit, start_line, end_line, pages. Tool-response leaves supply bytes; exitCode/exit_code/exit, is_error/isError, backgroundTaskId/session_id/sessionId/running/truncated/is_truncated control completion. [D1, D2, D4, D11, D20]
D22. Ledger output/read skin: given prompt bytes; own bytes; pending events/order and reservations; readings {q, subjects, texts, source, tool_use_id, tool, turn}; point_readings {path, point, started, tool_use_id}; mutations subject→q; code_changes {subject, display, aliases, data, data_form, record, q, tool_use_id}; executions {q, started, subjects, tool_use_id}; definitions {subject, definition}; point_aliases (pairs of normalized paths); unknown {q, reason}. Inputs/online/readbacks, shebangs and compiled products remain record projections; they cannot themselves pay lineage or substitute readback for execution. [D2, D4, D10, D11, D15, D16, D17, D18, D20, D21]
D23. Hook trigger: one JSON event on stdin. Stages: validate envelope, acquire per-session lock, verify hash-linked journal, replay earlier history, evaluate only dependent steps, ingest the event with admission status, append and fsync its journal row, encode response. Contract/transport exceptions end in a hold. Candidate reads never pay their own step. Owner: plugin/makoto2/hook.py main. [D1, D2, D14, D19, D20]
D24. Hook input skin: D21 plus transcript_path and stop_hook_active; configuration state_dir and adapter (only inferred). Validation additionally checks list shapes for makoto.obligations/dependencies and object shapes for destination/points; these are not claim evidence. Journal rows contain event, admitted, adapter, findings, contract_error, snapshot, turn_id, stop_hook_active_unpaid, unknown, previous and sha256. Findings have rule, family, shape, subject and missing; snapshots use borrowed.receipt. [D19, D21, D22, D23]
D25. Hook output skin: unpaid PreToolUse emits {"hookSpecificOutput":{"hookEventName":"PreToolUse","permissionDecision":"deny","permissionDecisionReason":reason}}; unpaid final emits {"decision":"block","reason":reason}; other successful events and successful finals emit {}. Admitted dependent PreToolUse emits hookSpecificOutput with hookEventName and additionalContext equal to hook.FOUR_QUESTIONS. A retry with stop_hook_active never suppresses an unpaid finding. The reason joins findings as `makoto rule <rule> [<shape>]: <JSON subject>: <missing>` separated by `; `. Errors use `makoto transport/contract failure: ` or `makoto contract: `. End: hold, empty response, context response and ledger record as applicable. [D19, D23, D24]
